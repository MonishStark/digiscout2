FAQ

Dining Questions

Do you offer plant-based milk options?

Yes, we proudly serve barista-grade oat, almond, and macadamia milks for any of our espresso or matcha beverages.

Is there reliable Wi-Fi for working?

Absolutely. High-speed fiber internet is available for all seated guests, and we provide dedicated work tables with access to outlets.

Do you take table reservations?

We operate purely on a walk-in basis, but with seating across two floors and an outdoor patio, you can usually find a comfortable spot.

Are your pastries vegan or gluten-free?

We offer a rotating daily selection of vegan and gluten-friendly baked goods. Please ask our baristas about today's specific offerings.
